# PHASE 15 — DESCRIPTIVE LEAGUE TRANSLATION INTELLIGENCE
## Empirical Transition Tracking across 8 Operational Dimensions

---

### 1. Multi-Dimensional Transition Tracking

When players transfer across competitions (e.g. Bundesliga $\to$ Premier League), the system tracks empirical shifts across 8 dimensions:

1. **Contribution**: Goal/shot creation, expected contributions per 90.
2. **Role Usage**: Shift in operational duties (e.g. touchline winger vs half-space forward).
3. **Minutes**: Playing time volume and starting XI share.
4. **Tactical Usage**: Pressing demands and possession involvement.
5. **Physical Workload**: High-intensity sprint frequency and distance covered.
6. **Availability**: Injury frequency and matchday availability percentage.
7. **Valuation Movement**: Post-transfer market valuation trajectory.
8. **Transfer Realization**: Fee vs performance delivery.

---

### 2. Evidence Statuses & Non-Causal Policy

For each dimension, the system classifies:
- **`OBSERVED_ASSOCIATION`**: Sufficient sample ($N \ge 10$) demonstrating consistent directional shift with P10–P90 uncertainty intervals.
- **`TRANSLATION_UNCERTAINTY`**: Moderate sample ($5 \le N < 10$) with wide dispersion.
- **`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`**: Fewer than 5 observed player transitions.

**Mandatory Doctrine**: The system never asserts that a competition "causes" a player to improve or deteriorate. All findings describe observational associations subject to team context, coaching styles, and individual adaptation variances.
